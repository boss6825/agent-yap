# Agentic RAG: Overview and Reading Order

These chapters explain the survey "Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation: A Survey on Agentic RAG" for someone who understands the basics of AI agents and has at least heard of [RAG](../glossary/Glossary.md#rag-retrieval-augmented-generation), but wants to understand how RAG grows up into something agentic.

## The article being explained

The folder contains one long markdown survey, plus a separate PDF ("Reasoning RAG via System 1 or System 2"). These chapters are based on the markdown survey, which is comprehensive: it covers the history of RAG, the building blocks of agentic intelligence, a full taxonomy of agentic RAG architectures, applications, and practical guidance.

Because it is one long document rather than several articles, these chapters break it into themes so you can read it in digestible pieces.

## The one idea behind everything

Ordinary RAG retrieves a fixed set of documents once and answers from them. That works for simple lookups but breaks on complex, multi-step questions. Agentic RAG adds [agents](../glossary/Glossary.md#agent) that can decide what to retrieve, retrieve repeatedly, judge the quality of what they got, and adapt, turning retrieval from a single snapshot into an active, reasoned process.
