## Recommended 2-Week Reading Order

### Week 1 — Foundations & Core Ideas
1. Watch: [3Blue1Brown Neural Network series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aircAruvnKk&list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi)
2. **Transformers** ⭐ → `01-Foundational-Modelling`
3. **GPT-3** ⭐ + **Scaling Laws** → `01-Foundational-Modelling`
4. **RLHF / InstructGPT** ⭐ → `01-Foundational-Modelling`
5. **Chain-of-Thought** ⭐ → `02-Planning-and-Reasoning`
6. **ReAct** → `02-Planning-and-Reasoning`
7. **LLM Survey** (skim) → Survey Papers

### Week 2 — Frontier & Applications
8. **LoRA** + **DPO** → `01-Foundational-Modelling`
9. **DeepSeek-R1** ⭐ → `02-Planning-and-Reasoning`
10. **Llama 3** ⭐ → `03-Applications`
11. **DeepSeek-V3** → `03-Applications`
12. **SWE-Agent** or **OpenHands** → `03-Applications`
13. **ARC Prize** ⭐ → `02-Planning-and-Reasoning`
14. **Chatbot Arena** + **SWE-Bench** → `04-Benchmarks`
15. **Agent Survey** (skim) → Survey Papers
