# 05 — Other (Non-LLM)

Foundational papers in computer vision and generative image modelling that are adjacent to LLMs but highly influential — especially for multimodal systems.

---

## Papers

| # | Title | Year | Link | Notes |
|---|-------|------|------|-------|
| 1 | **An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale** (ViT) | 2021 | [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.11929) | Applies pure Transformer (no CNNs) to image patches — dominant vision backbone today |
| 2 | **High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models** | 2021 | [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10752) | Text-to-image generation by running diffusion in latent space — basis of Stable Diffusion |

---

*Read ViT (1) if you want to understand how Transformers conquered vision; read Latent Diffusion (2) if you want to understand how image generation works.*
